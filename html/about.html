Our Story:

We are experienceINDIA, a passionate team dedicated to helping you discover the vibrant tapestry of India. We understand that travel is more than just ticking off destinations; it's about immersing yourself in the culture, savoring the flavors, and creating memories that will last a lifetime. Our journey began with a shared love for India's rich heritage and a desire to share its beauty with the world.

Why Choose Us?

We don't just offer itineraries; we craft experiences. From exploring ancient temples to experiencing the bustling street life, we curate authentic and unforgettable journeys tailored to your interests.

Our team comprises local experts who understand the nuances of India, allowing us to provide insider knowledge and genuine insights.

We believe in creating experiences that go beyond the ordinary. From cooking classes to yoga retreats, we offer a range of activities that will immerse you in the local culture.

We are committed to responsible tourism, partnering with local communities and businesses to ensure that your trip benefits the environment and the people of India.

Our Values:

  • Authenticity: We are committed to providing genuine and authentic experiences.
  • Responsibility: We believe in responsible tourism and preserving India's natural and cultural heritage.
  • Passion: We are passionate about India and dedicated to sharing its beauty with the world.
  • Excellence: We strive for excellence in every aspect of our service.
